Healthy Recipes

Simple meals to add nutrition to your day.

🥗

Fresh Vegetable Salad

Fresh vegetables with lemon dressing.

Calories: Approx. 150 kcal/serving

Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, carrot, lettuce, lemon.

Preparation: Chop the vegetables, mix together, and add lemon dressing.

🥣

Oatmeal Breakfast

A nourishing breakfast with fruit.

Calories: Approx. 300 kcal/serving

Ingredients: Oats, milk, banana, nuts, cinnamon.

Preparation: Cook oats with milk, then add sliced banana and nuts.

🍛

Vegetable Rice Bowl

A balanced meal with grains and vegetables.

Calories: Approx. 350 kcal/serving

Ingredients: Cooked rice, mixed vegetables, peas, and spices.

Preparation: Cook the vegetables with spices and serve with rice.

Note: Calorie values are estimates and vary with ingredients, portion sizes, and cooking methods.

Healthy Eating Tips

💧 Drink enough water throughout the day.
🥦 Include a variety of vegetables and fruits.
🌾 Choose whole grains more often.
🧂 Limit excess salt and added sugar.
🍽️ Eat balanced meals with suitable portions.
🏃 Combine healthy eating with regular activity.
